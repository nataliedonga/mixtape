Mixtape

Five tools. All of them work offline, and nothing you open leaves this device.

  1. 01

    Player

    Plays your own music files, with playlists, favourites and lyrics.

    Open
  2. 02

    Studio

    Cuts and mixes your songs on a timeline, with effects, instruments and split-out vocals.

    Open
  3. 03

    Cutter

    Cuts or joins any sound or video file, exact to the frame, whatever its format.

    Open
  4. 04

    Transcripts

    Turns a recording into words you can click to hear, correct and save.

    Open
  5. 05

    Reader

    Opens PDFs to read, mark up, fill in, sign and redact, and Word, text and ebook files to read.

    Open
