    Which pages to read

      You get a new PDF that looks the same: every page keeps its picture, and the words are laid over it as invisible text, so you can select, copy and search them. Pages that already have text are left exactly as they are. The reading is done on this device; nothing is uploaded. It is machine reading: it makes mistakes, and the result says how sure it was page by page.
